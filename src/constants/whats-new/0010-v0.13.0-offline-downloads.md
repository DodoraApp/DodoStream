# Offline downloads

You can now download movies and episodes on your phone and watch them without a connection.

- **Download:** tap the download button next to a stream in the stream selection
- **Whole seasons:** for a series, choose between the single episode and the whole season
- **In the background:** downloads continue while the app is in the background or the screen is locked (can be turned off in the settings under Downloads)
- **Find them again:** in the Library under Downloads and in the settings under Downloads
- **Limitations:** phones only, and only streams with a direct file link (no torrents, no HLS)
