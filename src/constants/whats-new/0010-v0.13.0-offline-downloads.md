# Offline downloads

You can now download movies and episodes on your phone and watch them without a connection.

- **Download:** tap the download button next to a stream in the stream selection
- **Whole seasons:** open the three-dots menu of a series and choose Download season
- **In the background:** downloads continue while the app is in the background or the screen is locked
- **After watching:** choose in the settings whether a watched download is deleted, kept, or whether the app asks
- **Find them again:** in the Library under Downloads, in the settings under Downloads, and through the download button on a title's details page
- **Limitations:** phones and tablets only, and only streams with a direct file link (no torrents, no HLS)
