# SIMKL integration

DodoStream can now sync your watch history and watchlist with SIMKL.

- **Two-way sync:** see what you watched in DodoStream and update your lists in SIMKL
- **Watchlist import & export:** bring your list over and keep it in sync
- **Sync modes:** choose import, export or full two-way sync
- **Connect once:** in the settings under Integrations
